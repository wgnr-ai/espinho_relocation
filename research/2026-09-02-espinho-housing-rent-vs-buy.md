## (a) RENTAL — Espinho

### Asking-rent distribution

1. Average asking rent in Espinho: **10,4 €/m²/month** (apartments 10,4 €/m²; houses not published), data month **August 2026**, +1,3% m/m, **+0,4% y/y** — rent growth is nearly flat. `[OFFICIAL, Idealista Espinho valuation page, retrieved 2026-09-02, https://www.idealista.pt/avaliacao-de-imoveis/espinho]`
2. Typical asking rents from live Idealista listings (retrieved 2026-09-02, https://www.idealista.pt/arrendar-casas/espinho/): `[PRACTICAL, Idealista listings, 2026-09-02]`
   - **T1**: 850–1.250 €/mês (most clustered 900–995 €; several in new Fosforeira/Wide River builds with garage and sea view)
   - **T2**: 900–2.300 €/mês (typical ~1.000–1.200 €; the 2.300 € outlier is a first-line luxury 138 m² unit)
   - **T3**: 1.150–2.050 €/mês (typical 1.200–1.500 €; incl. Silvalde 120 m² floor at 1.050 €)
   - **T6/luxury duplex**: 2.450 €/mês (central, sea view)
   - Studio/loft T0: ~1.025 € (temporary-rental segment, utilities included)
3. Cross-check vs district chatter: Aveiro-district rent commentary citing Espinho "can easily exceed 13 €/m²" for prime units `[PRACTICAL, Instagram reel citing district data, Mar 2026]` — consistent with the 2.300 € T2 outlier (≈16,7 €/m²); the 10,4 €/m² average remains the anchor.
4. Imovirtual does not publish an Espinho-specific €/m² figure in accessible results (national apartment rent average only: 2.406 € mean asking — distorted by luxury stock) `[PRACTICAL, Imovirtual results page, 2026-09-02]`. Casa.pt was not verifiable this session for Espinho rents — `[UNVERIFIED]`.

### Inventory depth

5. Idealista's Espinho rental page exposes roughly 30 listings on page 1 (mixed long-term and *arrendamento temporário*); the meta-description floor is "from 800 €" `[PRACTICAL, Idealista Espinho rentals, 2026-09-02]`.
6. Trovit (aggregator, includes multi-posted ads): ~165 T2 and ~162 T3 rental postings for the municipality, ~66 house rentals `[PRACTICAL, Trovit Espinho counts, 2026-09-02]`. Aggregates overstate unique stock; treat true depth as thin — a handful of suitable 2–3 bed long-term units visible at any time, with meaningful competition from the temporary/professional-relocation segment (several listings explicitly target "professores deslocados, obras em casa" with Oct 2026–Jun 2027 windows).

### Deposit, guarantor, and lease terms

7. Legal maximum security: landlords may require a **caução (deposit) of up to 2 months' rent** plus **advance payment of up to 2 rents**; more cannot be demanded `[OFFICIAL, NRAU/Código Civil as amended (Mais Habitação), via Diário da República consolidated legislation, retrieved 2026-09-02, https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/lei/2006-34578375]` and `[OFFICIAL, Idealista news, 2024-09-18, https://www.idealista.pt/news/imobiliario/habitacao/2024/09/18/65731-pagar-rendas-adiantadas-para-ter-desconto-o-que-diz-o-mercado-e-a-lei]`. So a realistic move-in cash requirement on a 1.200 € T2 is 2.400 € caução + first month(s), i.e., ~3.600–6.000 €.
8. Written contract is mandatory; contracts renew automatically unless opposed; rent updates happen under NRAU coefficients — 2026's update window is landlord-optional with a legal deadline `[OFFICIAL, NRAU via Diário da República, 2026-09-02]` + `[OFFICIAL, CGD rent-update explainer, retrieved 2026-09-02]`.
9. **Guarantor (fiador) reality**: the law allows landlords to demand a guarantor in lieu of/alongside deposit; in practice guarantors and renter's-insurance style guarantees are common for applicants without local income `[PRACTICAL, Aluga Seguro blog + Fórum da Casa threads, retrieved 2026-09-02]`. For the WGNRs: family in the area is the practical unlock — a local fiador (or proof of funds / advance rent within the legal cap) is the standard ask.
10. **"ARRE model contract"**: could not be verified this session — no Portuguese entity publishing a rental "model contract" under that name surfaced in official or market searches `[UNVERIFIED]`. The verified frame: lease content is governed by the NRAU (Lei 6/2006 as amended), and ready-made *minutas* circulate from law firms and DECO `[OFFICIAL, NRAU via Diário da República, 2026-09-02]` + `[PRACTICAL, CJA minuta PDF + DECO rental guides, retrieved 2026-09-02]`. Action: request an NRAU-compliant contract; do not assume an "ARRE" template exists until a source is produced.
11. **Lei 9-A/2026 (6 March 2026)** is a *fiscal supply-side* law — it cuts IRS/IRC on residential rents (up to ~2.300 €), drops construction IVA 23%→6%, and creates incentives to build/rehabilitate — it does **not** change deposits or guarantor rules `[OFFICIAL, Diário da República Lei 9-A/2026 + Decreto-Lei 97/2026 (20 May 2026), retrieved 2026-09-02]` + `[OFFICIAL, Comunicado do Conselho de Ministros, 2026-03-27, portugal.gov.pt/gc25/governo/comunicados-do-conselho-de-ministros/719]`. Effect for renters: more supply pressure released through 2026–2029, i.e., mild medium-term relief, not a rule change.

### Furnished vs unfurnished, accessibility, pets

12. Furnished vs unfurnished split (live listings): a large share of central/near-beach 1–2 bed stock is **mobilado e equipado** (often targeting temporary tenants); family-sized T3+ and the T6 segment skew unfurnished ("não está mobilado" explicitly stated on the T6) `[PRACTICAL, Idealista listings, 2026-09-02]`. For a long-term T2–T3 household move, expect to inspect both and to furnish if the unit is unfurnished.
13. Accessibility/pet attitudes: no listing-level pet or accessibility policies are published on Idealista Espinho listings; no municipal policy verified — `[UNVERIFIED]`. Practical note (unverified): pet acceptance and elevator/step-free access are negotiated case-by-case with landlords; several inspected buildings state "com elevador" which matters for Leo.

### Process for PT citizens returning from abroad

14. Documents: NIF (citizen right — obtainable/revalidated at Finanças with Cartão de Cidadão), Portuguese bank account (openable remotely/in-person at CGD/BCP/Novo Banco; CGD and BCP maintain dedicated emigrant channels), and standard ID `[OFFICIAL, CGD Residentes no Estrangeiro page + Millennium BCP Portugueses no Exterior page, retrieved 2026-09-02]`. No visa/immigration layer applies — citizens.
15. The hard part in practice: landlords/agents favor applicants with local income; returning citizens with US income commonly compensate via fiador (family), advance rent within the legal cap, or broker platforms (Aluga Seguro) `[PRACTICAL, Aluga Seguro + Fórum da Casa, 2026-09-02]`. Cash-ready applications move fastest in this market.

## (b) PURCHASE — Espinho

### Median/average asking €/m² and trend

1. Average asking price Espinho: **2.816 €/m² all homes; 3.208 €/m² apartments; 2.111 €/m² houses**, data month **August 2026**, **+9,2% y/y**, +2,1% m/m `[OFFICIAL, Idealista Espinho valuation page, retrieved 2026-09-02]`.
2. Listing-page cross-checks: Idealista comprar-casas/espinho page showed average **3.158 €/m²** in results `[PRACTICAL, Idealista sale search snapshot, Sept 2026]`; Mitula (aggregating Idealista feeds) shows **3.479 €/m²** avg with +8,2% June-2026 evolution `[PRACTICAL, Mitula Espinho, retrieved 2026-09-02]`; Properstar median apartment **3.909 €/m²** `[PRACTICAL, Properstar Espinho, retrieved 2026-09-02]`. Convergence: realistic apartment asking band **~3.200–3.900 €/m²**; i.e., a 100 m² T2/T3 asks roughly **320.000–390.000 €** (example listing: T3 205 m² at 450.000 € = 2.195 €/m² for larger units; T4 144 m² at 645.000 € = 4.479 €/m² near-prime `[PRACTICAL, Idealista listings via search, 2026-09-02]`).
3. Trend vs 12 months: **+9,2% y/y** (Espinho, Aug 2026) `[OFFICIAL, Idealista, 2026-09-02]`. Context: national transaction median 2.337 €/m² Q1 2026, +19,8% y/y `[OFFICIAL, INE local house-price statistics via Portugal Homes summary, 2026-07-17]`; national asking-price growth decelerated through 2026 (Aug +7,5% y/y) `[OFFICIAL, Idealista price index news, Sept 2026]`; North region +5,5% y/y as of June 2026 `[OFFICIAL, Idealista index via GrowIN Portugal, 2026-07-17]`.
4. Trend vs 24 months: Espinho-specific two-year figure `[UNVERIFIED]` — no published Espinho 24-month series was retrievable. Proxy only: national prices roughly +25–35% over two years in active markets `[PRACTICAL, Investropa housing-prices analysis, 2026-06-16]`. Directionally Espinho rose strongly but more moderately than the national median.

### Full transaction-cost breakdown (buyer side)

5. **IMT 2026 brackets — habitação própria e permanente (Continental)** `[OFFICIAL, IMT tables in force 2026-01-01, source notarios.pt via CAFIMO simulator page, retrieved 2026-09-02, https://cafimo.pt/simulador-imt/]`:

| Price | Rate | Amount to deduct |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ 106.346 € | exempt | — |
| 106.346,01–145.470 € | 2% | 2.126,92 € |
| 145.470,01–198.347 € | 5% | 6.491,02 € |
| 198.347,01–330.539 € | 7% | 10.457,96 € |
| 330.539,01–660.982 € | 8% | 13.763,35 € |
| 660.982,01–1.150.853 € | 6% | — |
| > 1.150.853 € | 7,5% | — |

   Secondary-home/letting brackets differ only in the abatement column (e.g., 2% band deducts 1.063,46 €; 8% band runs to 633.931 €) `[OFFICIAL, same CAFIMO/notarios.pt tables, 2026-09-02]`. Note: the 6%/7,5% top bands produce marginal regressivity — a ~350 k€ primary residence sits near the effective-cost peak.
6. **Imposto de Selo (stamp duty) on purchase: 0,8%** of deed value or tax-assessed value (whichever higher) `[OFFICIAL, CAFIMO/notarios.pt 2026 + Doutor Finanças IMT/IS pages, retrieved 2026-09-02]`. A separate Imposto de Selo also applies on the mortgage loan (rate not re-verified this session — `[UNVERIFIED]`).
7. Worked example — **350.000 € apartment, primary residence** (computed from the cited brackets): IMT = 350.000×8% − 13.763,35 = **14.236,65 €**; IS = **2.800 €**; notary/registration ≈ **550–1.400 €** `[PRACTICAL, Savory & Partners fee ranges: notary 300–1.000 €, registry 250–400 €, 2026-04-27]` to ≈ **1.000–3.000 €** `[PRACTICAL, Investropa, 2026-06-16]`. **Total ≈ 17.600–20.000 € (~5,0–5,7%)** on top of price. At 450.000 €: IMT 22.236,65 € + IS 3.600 € + fees ≈ **26.300–28.800 € (~5,8–6,4%)** (computed).
8. **Buyer agent fees**: seller customarily pays 3–5% commission to the listing agent; buyer-side representation is negotiable (flat/hourly), and Portugal has no mandatory buyer-agent fee `[PRACTICAL, Savory & Partners + Investropa, 2026]`. Budget for optional lawyer/due diligence ≈ 0,5–1,5% (≈1.000–2.000 € typical) `[PRACTICAL, Savory & Partners, 2026-04-27]`.
9. Annual ownership cost note: IMI 0,3–0,45% of tax-assessed value for urban residential `[OFFICIAL, IMI rates via CAFIMO page citing municipal setting, 2026-09-02]`.

### Mortgage availability — returning PT citizens with US income

10. Banks explicitly serve this profile: **Novo Banco** non-resident mortgage product — variable rate Euribor 12M + spread from 0,8%, for own/secondary/rental purchase `[OFFICIAL, novobanco.pt estrangeiros-nao-residentes page, retrieved 2026-09-02]`; **CGD** maintains Residentes-no-Estrangeiro solutions including credit `[OFFICIAL, cgd.pt page, retrieved 2026-09-02]`; **Millennium BCP** maintains its Portugueses-residentes-no-exterior channel `[OFFICIAL, millenniumbcp.pt page, retrieved 2026-09-02]`.
11. Portuguese banks **accept foreign-source income if documented** (contracts, tax returns, bank statements) `[PRACTICAL, ComparaJá emigrantes page, retrieved 2026-09-02]`. In practice, applicants living abroad are asked for **20–30% down**; 100% financing is not available on that track `[PRACTICAL, ComparaJá + r/literaciafinanceira practitioner thread, 2026]`.
12. Typical LTV/rates 2026: residents up to 80–90% LTV; non-resident/foreign-income files typically capped ~60–70% (some sources say up to 80%) `[PRACTICAL, Savory & Partners, 2026-04-27]`. Fixed rates ~3,5–4,2%; variable ~3–3,8% `[PRACTICAL, Savory & Partners, 2026-04-27]`. ECB deposit rate 2,25% (June 2026) underpins firm-but-stable pricing `[OFFICIAL, ECB decision June 2026 via Investropa, 2026-06-19]`.
13. Timing matters for the WGNRs: applying **before establishing PT residency = non-resident track** (lower LTV, heavier documentation). Applying **after relocation + PT-resident status with US pension/income declared in Portugal** shifts them toward resident terms `[PRACTICAL, synthesis of bank pages above, 2026-09-02]`. IMT Jovem (≤35 exemption) is irrelevant to this family `[OFFICIAL, IMT Jovem rules via CAFIMO, 2026-09-02]`.

### Realistic time-to-close

14. End-to-end purchase in Portugal: **2–6 months, 3–4 months most common** `[PRACTICAL, SUPERCASA guide, 2025-06-18]`; CPCV→escritura interval commonly **~90 days** when financing is involved `[PRACTICAL, Engel & Völkers CPCV guide + literaciafinanceira 2026 approval-timeline article, retrieved 2026-09-02]`. Bank credit approval itself: ~2–3 weeks typical in the 2026 example timeline `[PRACTICAL, literaciafinanceira.pt, 2026-09]`. For a Jan 2027 arrival, a spring 2027 deed is realistic if the search starts on arrival.

## Breakeven intuition

- Espinho price-to-rent: 3.208 €/m² ÷ (10,4 €/m² × 12) ≈ **25,7 years** of rent to equal purchase price (computed from cited averages) — a **gross rental yield ~3,9%**. Espinho is the most expensive of the three markets *relative to its rents*: SMF ≈ 5,5% yield (~18,2 yr) and Ovar ≈ 5,1% (~19,8 yr) (computed from cited figures).
- Simple rule for the family: at ~26× annual rent, buying in Espinho only beats renting if (a) holding ≥5–7 years, (b) prices keep appreciating near recent rates, and (c) mortgage+costs don't exceed ~1,5× the alternative rent for long. Renting in Espinho is comparatively "cheap" against purchase prices; buying is comparatively better value one municipality inland, where the same logic yields ~18–20×.

## What would tip the decision

**Toward RENT (Espinho, first 6–12 months):** arrival without PT credit history or residency-based income assessment (non-resident LTV 60–70% and 20–30% down); thin long-term inventory argues for taking the best available unit fast; rents are flat (+0,4% y/y) so waiting costs little; Lei 9-A/2026 supply incentives may add stock and soften rents through 2027–2029; flexibility to test Espinho vs Esmoriz/SMF before committing ~350 k€ + ~5–6% friction.

**Toward BUY:** certainty of permanence ≥5–7 years; age-in-place control for Leo's care (adapt accessibility, no landlord/contract risk); ability to wait until PT residency + declared income unlocks resident LTV (80–90%) and better spreads; willingness to buy in **Ovar/Esmoriz/SMF** where yields ~5%+, prices 20–40% lower, and Ovar's +21,6% y/y momentum suggests the value gap is already closing; mortgage rates stable-to-favorable (ECB 2,25%, Novo Banco spreads from 0,8%).
