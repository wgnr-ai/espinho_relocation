## (a) US → EU Pet Import Requirements

### A.1 Microchip

1. **ISO-compliant 15-digit microchip** (ISO 11784 / ISO 11785) is required. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS, retrieved 2026-09-03 — see URL above]
2. **The microchip must be implanted BEFORE the rabies vaccination.** A rabies shot administered before microchipping (or before the microchip is scanned) is **invalid for EU entry** — the vaccination must be repeated, and the 21-day waiting clock restarts. This is "the single most common and costly sequencing mistake." [PRACTICAL, Across the Pond Pets 2026-06-04, retrieved 2026-09-03]
3. If Leo already has a non-ISO microchip, options are: travel with a scanner that reads it, OR have a vet implant an ISO-compliant chip. Both numbers must be listed on the health certificate. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS, retrieved 2026-09-03]
4. **Action item:** confirm Leo's microchip status and ISO compliance NOW; if a chip is needed or replaced, schedule before any rabies action.

### A.2 Rabies vaccination

5. Administered **after** the microchip scan, by an authorized veterinarian. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS, retrieved 2026-09-03]
6. **21-day waiting period after primary vaccination** (or the manufacturer's recommended immunity period — some say 30 days). The vet must record the correct waiting period on the rabies certificate; it determines validity dates. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS, retrieved 2026-09-03]
7. A **primary** rabies vaccination is valid for **1 year** for EU travel purposes, even if the US-labelled vaccine is a 3-year product. A **booster** within 12 months keeps validity at the manufacturer's 1–3 year label and **removes the 21-day wait**. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS, retrieved 2026-09-03]
8. **Single rabies certificate rule:** USDA APHIS recommends a fresh 1-year rabies vaccine given **3–6 months before travel** for the cleanest paperwork. Multiple historical rabies certificates (showing no lapse) are also acceptable. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS, retrieved 2026-09-03]
9. **Action item:** if Leo's current rabies is overdue or predates microchipping, plan a fresh 1-year rabies shot at least 21 days before the endorsed health certificate can be issued — ideally several months before the move to keep the paperwork clean.

### A.3 Tapeworm treatment — NOT REQUIRED FOR PORTUGAL

10. The Echinococcus tapeworm treatment (praziquantel, 24–120 hours before entry) is **required only for dogs entering Finland, Malta, Ireland (including Northern Ireland), or Norway**. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS — Pet Travel From the United States to Finland, Malta, Ireland (including Northern Ireland), or Norway, retrieved 2026-09-03] [PRACTICAL, Across the Pond Pets 2026-06-04, retrieved 2026-09-03]
11. **Portugal is NOT in the tapeworm-treatment list.** No praziquantel step is required for entry via Lisbon (LIS) or any Portuguese airport.

### A.4 USDA-endorsed health certificate

12. There are two certificates: **non-commercial (EU 2026/131)** and **commercial (EU 2026/848)**. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS, retrieved 2026-09-03]
13. **Non-commercial** applies when the **owner or a designated person travels on the same flight** (or within 5 days after the owner) with **5 or fewer pets**, and there is no ownership transfer. This covers Wagner's case — Wagner flies with Leo on the same itinerary. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS, retrieved 2026-09-03]
14. **Commercial** applies when the pet travels without an accompanying person, when ownership transfers, or for 6+ pets. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS, retrieved 2026-09-03]
15. The non-commercial certificate must be **issued by a USDA-accredited veterinarian within 30 days** of issuance and **USDA must endorse it within 10 days** of the pet's arrival in the EU. The owner-declaration section (final page) must travel with the pet. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS, retrieved 2026-09-03]
16. Vet visit window for the certificate: the AHC must be issued **before the flight** with enough lead time for USDA endorsement. USDA endorsement itself takes roughly **3–5 business days** (industry rule of thumb; not from a live APHIS page this session). [PRACTICAL, Across the Pond Pets 2026-06-04, retrieved 2026-09-03] **[UNVERIFIED for exact 2026 APHIS turnaround — confirm at booking]**
17. The endorsed certificate must be the **new EU 2026/131 format** — old certificates are not endorsable after 30 September 2026. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS, retrieved 2026-09-03]
18. The certificate must travel with Leo (printed copy; physical originals plus digital backups recommended) and be presented at the travellers' point of entry. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS, retrieved 2026-09-03]

### A.5 EU pet passport — issued on arrival

19. The **EU pet passport** is issued by an authorised veterinarian or customs/border official **inside the EU**, typically at the first point of entry. It is **not** issued before travel and is **not** available to non-EU residents for the entry trip itself. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS, retrieved 2026-09-03]
20. For Wagner's April–June 2027 arrival, the **sequence is**: US side — microchip → rabies → AHC (EU 2026/131) → USDA endorsement; EU side — present AHC at LIS customs → passport issued by an authorised local vet or border official. [OFFICIAL, USDA APHIS, retrieved 2026-09-03]
21. **Action item:** confirm whether LIS issues the passport at the airport itself or requires visiting a designated vet. **[UNVERIFIED — Lisbon airport procedure not specifically retrieved this session; flagged for confirmation]**

### A.6 Other 2026 enforcement points

22. The 2026 framework explicitly requires the **owner or designated person to travel on the same flight** for non-commercial classification; pet-nanny / pet-transport services that fly separately trigger commercial classification. [PRACTICAL, Across the Pond Pets 2026-06-04, retrieved 2026-09-03]
23. Maximum **5 dogs/cats/ferrets per person** in a single non-commercial movement. [PRACTICAL, Across the Pond Pets 2026-06-04, retrieved 2026-09-03]
24. **No breed restrictions for entry to Portugal** at EU level (Germany's Länder-level breed rules are a different question and not relevant here). [PRACTICAL, Across the Pond Pets 2026-06-04 — comparison table, retrieved 2026-09-03]

[PRACTICAL — multiple 2024–2026 expat guides, retrieved 2026-09-03] **[UNVERIFIED — primary text of Lei 81/2014 not retrieved this session; verify with PT lawyer or DECO]**
