## Q1 — US SOCIAL SECURITY & FEDERAL BENEFITS FOR PT-RESIDENT US SENIORS

### 1.1 Persons in scope
- **Father of Wagner** — US senior, US citizen, relocating to Espinho
- **Leo dos Santos** — US senior, relocating to Espinho
- Both are US Social Security beneficiaries (or about to claim)

### 1.2 Are Social Security payments uninterrupted when relocating to Portugal? [OFFICIAL]

**Answer: YES, uninterrupted.**

- Portugal is **NOT** on SSA's list of restricted countries (Country List 1) where the agency is barred from sending payments. [OFFICIAL, https://www.ssa.gov/international/countrylist1.htm, verified via search results 2026-09-03]
- "Portugal is not one of the countries with direct deposit agreements. You have basically two options: keep your US bank account and withdraw money abroad, or use the Direct Express debit card program." — [PRACTICAL, Claimyr 2025-03-27]
- **However, Portugal IS a country with which the US has an International Social Security (Totalization) Agreement** (see §1.6 below). Under Article 3 of the agreement, citizens and residents of Portugal are no longer subject to the SSA nonpayment provision that would otherwise apply to aliens who first became eligible after 1984 and are outside the US for more than 6 months. [OFFICIAL, https://www.ssa.gov/international/PortugalAnnFile/PortAgreeArticle%203%202.html]

**Implication:** As US citizens, father and Leo are not subject to the 6-month rule for aliens. Payments are uninterrupted from day one of move.

### 1.3 Direct-deposit requirements — country-specific Portugal form [OFFICIAL]

There is a **dedicated Portugal-specific SSA-1199-PE** form ("Direct Deposit Sign-Up Form – Portugal"). The form is filled out, Section 3 is completed by the PT bank, and the entire form is mailed/faxed/emailed to the Lisbon Federal Benefits Unit (FBU) at the US Embassy. [OFFICIAL, https://www.ssa.gov/forms/ssa-1199-pe.pdf — form dated 02-2021; verified via Valyu 2026-09-03]

**Key facts:**
- US Treasury converts the USD benefit to EUR at the **daily international exchange rate** before depositing into the PT bank account. [OFFICIAL, SSA-1199-PE form text]
- Father and Leo do NOT need to keep a US bank account just for Social Security. Direct deposit into a Portuguese bank (any Portugal-based bank with routing/SWIFT-compatible rails) is supported.
- **mySocialSecurity does NOT allow updates to bank accounts outside the US.** Foreign-bank DD changes must go through the Lisbon FBU via email (fbu.lisbon@ssa.gov) or in person. [OFFICIAL, https://pt.usembassy.gov/social-security/, verified 2026-09-03]
- Effective April 14, 2025, SSA no longer establishes identity verification over the phone for direct-deposit changes. [OFFICIAL, US Embassy PT page]

**Lisbon Federal Benefits Unit contact** [OFFICIAL]:

### 1.4 Other SSA forms & ongoing requirements after move [OFFICIAL]

- **SSA-21** ("Supplement To Claim Of Person Outside The United States") — required when reporting a new foreign address to SSA. Submit by mail or to fbu.lisbon@ssa.gov. [OFFICIAL, US Embassy PT]
- **Form 8822** — change-of-address for IRS (which feeds SSA's address record). Mail to IRS. [OFFICIAL, US Embassy PT]
- **Foreign Enforcement Questionnaires (SSA-7161 / SSA-7162)** — MAILED EACH YEAR IN MAY. Failure to return = benefits suspended effective February 3. **This is the most common reason expat seniors lose payments.** [OFFICIAL, US Embassy PT]
  - Forms are mailed to a US facility in Wilkes-Barre, PA (not to the FBU). Return envelope provided.
  - **Action item:** Set annual reminder (May) for both father and Leo.
- **WEP/GPO — REPEALED.** The Social Security Fairness Act (HR 82) was signed into law on January 5, 2025. WEP and GPO reductions are eliminated prospectively and retroactively. SSA is implementing as of 2026; SSA-1099/1042S statements reflect corrected amounts. [OFFICIAL, US Embassy PT page]
  - **This is a major win** if either senior previously had benefits reduced by WEP/GPO. They may be entitled to retroactive lump sums.

### 1.5 Federal income tax on SSA benefits for a US citizen abroad [OFFICIAL]

- **US citizens are taxed on worldwide income regardless of residence** (IRC §1; §61). [OFFICIAL]
- Social Security benefits are partially taxable at the federal level (up to 85% under IRC §86), based on "combined income" (AGI + tax-exempt interest + ½ of SS benefits).
- **SS benefits are NOT foreign-earned income** — they are not excluded by the Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (FEIE, IRC §911). A US person abroad who claims FEIE must still include SS benefits in taxable income (though up to 85% taxable, not 100%). [PRACTICAL, taxesforexpats.com 2026]
- Filing posture choices for the seniors:
  1. **Use the Foreign Tax Credit (Form 1116)** — typically no excess credit because Portugal taxes SS differently. [PRACTICAL]
  2. **Totalization Agreement with Portugal (see §1.6)** — relevant only if the senior works; for retirees receiving only SS, totalization provides coordination, not double-tax relief.
- **Bottom line:** Father and Leo must file Form 1040 each year, include 0–85% of SS in taxable income based on combined-income thresholds, and claim the standard deduction or itemized deductions. They should consult a US expat tax preparer (GreenbackTax, H&R Block Expat, etc.). [PRACTICAL]

### 1.6 US-Portugal Totalization Agreement — status today [OFFICIAL]

**CONFIRMED IN FORCE:**
- **Agreement signed:** March 30, 1988
- **Effective date:** August 1, 1989
- **Legal citation:** TIAS 12121

[OFFICIAL, https://www.ssa.gov/international/status.html — verified 2026-09-03 via Valyu extract]

**What it does:**
1. **Eliminates dual Social Security coverage** — workers pay into only one country's system. A US employee seconded to PT for ≤ 5 years stays in US Social Security (employer requests certificate P/USA 1 from PT social security). [OFFICIAL, SSA Agreement Pamphlet Portugal]
2. **Coordinates benefit eligibility** — periods of coverage in one country can be "totalized" to qualify for benefits in the other. Minimum requirement: 6 US credits (≈ 1.5 years) for US to count PT credits; 12 months PT coverage for PT to count US credits. [OFFICIAL, SSA Agreement Pamphlet Portugal]
3. **Removes the SSA nonpayment provision for PT citizens/residents** eligible after 1984 and outside the US > 6 months. [OFFICIAL, SSA Article 3.2 commentary]

**Practical impact for father & Leo:**
- If either was self-employed in the US and never paid SE tax, they may have < 40 US credits. Totalization can use PT credits (if they later accumulate 12 months PT coverage) to qualify for partial US benefits.
- If they worked in Portugal before the 1989 effective date, those pre-1989 periods count toward eligibility. [OFFICIAL, POMS GN 01727.001]
- **Apply for benefits under the Agreement** using **Form SSA-2490-BK** ("Application for Benefits Under a U.S. International Social Security Agreement"). [OFFICIAL, SSA Agreement Pamphlet]

## Q2 — RUNNING A US BUSINESS FROM PORTUGAL (WAGNER)

### 2.1 Personal tax residency shift — when does the US consider Wagner no longer a US tax resident?

**Short answer: Almost never.** US citizens are taxed on worldwide income regardless of residence (IRC §1; §61). The only way to cease US tax residency is to:
1. Renounce US citizenship (with expatriation tax under §877A — heavy)
2. Die
3. Have a "non-US citizen, non-green-card holder" status (Wagner is a US citizen by birth — does not apply)

[OFFICIAL, IRC §1, §61, §7701(b) — verified via practitioner guides 2026]

**The FEIE doesn't end US tax residency — it excludes a portion of foreign-earned income from US tax.**

### 2.2 FEIE (Foreign Earned Income Exclusion) and the closer-connection exception

Wagner remains a US tax resident and must file Form 1040 every year. FEIE eligibility requires either:
- **Bona fide residence test** — bona fide resident of a foreign country for an uninterrupted period including an entire tax year [OFFICIAL, https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-earned-income-exclusion-bona-fide-residence-test]
- **Physical presence test** — 330 full days outside the US in any 12-month period [OFFICIAL, IRS Pub 54]

**2026 FEIE exclusion amount [UNVERIFIED — IRS Pub 54 is canonical]:**
- Most practitioner guides state **$132,900** for 2026 [PRACTICAL, taxpayers.net, yourtaxbase.com, countrytaxcalc.com]
- One source states **$126,500** for 2026 [PRACTICAL, tax-clean.com]
- The IRS adjusts annually for inflation; the actual 2026 figure is published in Rev. Proc. typically in late fall of the prior year. **[ACTION: verify on IRS.gov Rev. Proc. 2025-XX]**

**For Wagner's transition year (2027 — move Apr–Jun):**
- He cannot meet the **bona fide residence test** in 2027 (must include an entire tax year). He will first qualify in tax year 2028.
- He CAN meet the **physical presence test** for the partial year — needs 330 full days outside the US within a 12-month period that overlaps 2027. Plan the move so the 12-month window satisfies the test (e.g., move by April 1, 2027 to capture April–Dec 2027 + early 2028).

### 2.3 Closer connection exception (IRC §911(c)(1)(B))

If Wagner meets the physical presence test but the IRS determines he has a "closer connection to the US than to the foreign country," he is **barred from FEIE**.

The IRS looks at factors like:
- Length of stay abroad vs US
- Tax home location (where you work; where you maintain an abode)
- Family ties in US (US-resident spouse, minor children)
- Personal items, vehicles, social/political/cultural ties
- Banking, business, driver's license location

[OFFICIAL, IRS Pub 54 §911(c); closer connection exception explained in Treasury Reg. §1.911-2(d)(2)]

**For Wagner:** Strong ties to US business + family + banking could trigger the exception. Plan: cut US ties aggressively (close US bank if not needed, surrender US driver's license, register car in PT, register on PT voter rolls as resident, join PT social/cultural organizations). Document with Form 8898 (filing required in year of residency change).

### 2.4 US-PT Tax Treaty (signed 1994) — tie-breaker

**Citation:** Convention Between the Government of the United States of America and the Government of the Portuguese Republic for the Avoidance of Double Taxation, signed September 6, 1994. [OFFICIAL, https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/portugal.pdf; https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/Treaty-Portugal-TE-9-6-1994.pdf]

**Article 4 — Residence tie-breaker (applicable to dual residents):**
1. Permanent home available in one country only → that country
2. If permanent home in both or neither → personal & economic relations ("center of vital interests")
3. If still tied → habitual abode (where the person lives more frequently)
4. If still tied → nationality
5. If still tied → competent authorities of both countries settle by mutual agreement

[OFFICIAL, IRS Treaty Portugal PDF, Article 4(2)(a)–(d) — verified via search 2026-09-03]

**Critical wrinkle:** Per the Protocol to the treaty, Portugal will treat a US citizen or green-card holder as a US resident for treaty purposes ONLY IF:
- The person has substantial US presence, OR
- The person would not be considered a resident of a third country under the Article 4 tie-breaker

[PRACTICAL, https://mcs.pt/us-portugal-tax-treaty-explained-7-essential-insights-for-americans-in-portugal/, 2026]

**Implication for Wagner:** Once he's fully relocated to PT (no third-country residence), the treaty tie-breaker becomes irrelevant for his PT-tax purposes — he's a PT tax resident. The treaty is mainly relevant for:
- US-source income paid to him (withholding rates reduced)
- Avoiding double-tax on the same income
- Capital gains from PT real estate sales
- Pension/annuity treatment

### 2.5 PT personal income tax (IRS Portugal) — NHR successor (IFICI)

**NHR closed Dec 31, 2023.** Replaced by **IFICI (Incentivo Fiscal à Investigação Científica e Inovação)**, effective Jan 1, 2024, created by **Lei n.º 82/2023** (Portugal State Budget 2024). [PRACTICAL, Lexunion, Sable International, RFF Lawyers — verified 2026]

**IFICI status in 2026 [PRACTICAL]:**
- Active and **accepting new registrations** through **15 January** of the year following the year in which the applicant becomes PT tax resident
- First wave of IFICI approvals issued by Portuguese Tax Authority in **March 2026** — confirming the regime is operational
- Eligibility: **highly qualified professionals** in scientific research & innovation; advanced degree required; accredited by relevant government agency (e.g., IAPMEI, AICEP, ANI)
- Tax benefit: **20% flat rate** on eligible Portuguese-source employment/self-employment income (vs. progressive 14.5%–48% + 2.5%–5% solidarity for ordinary IRS)
- Foreign-source income: still taxable in PT (subject to ordinary rules + double-tax treaty relief); IFICI does NOT exempt foreign income in the same way NHR did
- 10-year benefit period

[PRACTICAL, https://www.expertsforexpats.com/advice/tax/nhr-2-ifici-in-portugal; https://www.sableinternational.com/portugal-nhr-2.0; https://immigrantinvest.com/blog/portugal-ifici-regime/ — all 2026]

**Wagner's likely IFICI category [UNVERIFIED — verify with PT tax advisor]:**
- "**Empregador científico**" (scientific employment) — applies if Wagner is a researcher/professor
- "**Atividades de valor acrescentado**" (value-added activities) — applies to certain skilled activities listed in the law (IT, engineering, R&D, etc.)
- "**Trabalhador qualificado em setor estratégico**" — if working for a Portuguese employer in a strategic sector
- **Most likely NOT applicable** if Wagner's work is general consulting, marketing, sales, agency work, or non-STEM consulting. IFICI is narrow by design.

**Default if Wagner doesn't qualify:** Falls into **ordinary PT personal income tax (IRS)**:
- Progressive rates: 14.5% (up to €7,478) → 23% → 28.5% → 35% → 37.75% (over €80,882), plus 2.5%–5% solidarity surcharge above €80,000
- Worldwide income taxed (with double-tax treaty relief)

### 2.6 PT corporate tax — does a US LLC/C-corp managed from PT trigger a PT permanent establishment (PE)?

**Short answer: HIGH RISK for a US LLC managed from PT; possible for a US C-corp.** [PRACTICAL, https://mcs.pt/us-llcs-and-portuguese-tax-how-portugal-characterises-your-llc-2026-guide/; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/portugal/corporate/corporate-residence — 2026]

**Key tests under PT law (CIRC art. 2; art. 4) and OECD Model Tax Convention Article 5:**
1. **Effective management test (place of central management):** If the LLC's board/manager makes decisions from PT, the LLC is PT-resident (treated as a PT company for tax purposes — full Portuguese corporate obligations).
2. **Fixed place of business PE:** If Wagner keeps an office, equipment, or a "disponible" desk in PT used for the business.
3. **Dependent agent PE:** If Wagner (or any PT-based agent) **habitually** concludes contracts or plays the principal role in contract negotiation in PT — even without a fixed office, this creates PE. [OFFICIAL, OECD MC Art. 5(5)]
4. **Independent agent carve-out:** Independent agents acting in the ordinary course of business do NOT create PE — but they must genuinely be independent (not 100% dependent on the principal).
5. **Preparation/auxiliary carve-out:** Pure preparation, storage, or display activities do NOT create PE.

[OFFICIAL, OECD MC Art. 5(4)–(5); US-PT Treaty Article 5 — confirmed via treaty PDF]

**Practical PE triggers for Wagner running a US LLC from PT:**
| Activity | Risk |
|---|---|
| Wagner personally negotiates/signs contracts with US clients from PT | HIGH (dependent agent PE) |
| Wagner holds video calls from PT to close deals | MEDIUM (PE risk depends on substance) |
| Wagner has PT bank account in the LLC's name used for client payments | MEDIUM (PT source-rule risk) |
| LLC has a registered office in PT (virtual office) | HIGH (fixed place of business) |
| US co-founder/manager also makes decisions from PT | HIGH (effective management) |
| Wagner invoices from PT using PT address | HIGH |
| Wagner merely operates from a home office in PT with no client-facing activity | LOWER (preparation/auxiliary carve-out may apply, but substance matters) |

**Source-rule analysis:** If the LLC's income is treated as Wagner's personal services income sourced in PT (because Wagner performs the services from PT), PT personal income tax (IRS, progressive) applies. If the LLC is treated as PT-resident (effective management), PT corporate tax (IRC art. 4; 21% corporate rate in 2026) applies on worldwide income — **the most punitive outcome.**

**Modelo 22:** PT corporate tax return. If the US LLC is treated as PT tax resident (effective management) OR has a PE in PT, Wagner files Modelo 22 annually. [PRACTICAL, PwC tax summaries 2026]

**Three entity model options for PT-resident founders of US businesses:**

| Model | Setup | PT tax outcome | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(A) Continue US entity, accept PT PE registration** | File Modelo 22 for PT PE branch | LLC's PT-source income taxed at 21% PT corporate + 25% IRS on dividends | Clients mostly US; PT presence is incidental |
| **(B) Create a Portuguese company (Lda or SA) with a US branch** | PT company is the operating entity; US branch handles US clients | PT company taxed on worldwide at 21%; US branch profits taxed in US | Substantial PT operations planned |
| **(C) Continue US entity as foreign-owned, disregarded for PT tax** | LLC treated as fiscally transparent; Wagner reports income on his PT personal Modelo 3 | Income flows through to Wagner's PT progressive tax rates + IRS rate for US-source items | Small volume; Wagner is the sole operator; **UNVERIFIED whether AT will accept this without a binding ruling** |

[PRACTICAL, https://fresh-legal.com/blog/the-tax-treatment-of-us-llcs-in-portugal; https://mcs.pt/us-llcs-and-portuguese-tax-how-portugal-characterises-your-llc-2026-guide/ — 2026]

**Recommendation:** Before the move, obtain a **binding ruling** from Autoridade Tributária (AT) on how the LLC will be treated — or — restructure the entity.

### 2.7 VAT (IVA) — does Wagner charge PT IVA on US-business invoices?

**Short answer: Generally NO, with caveats.**

**Place-of-supply rules (CIVA — Código do Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado):**
- **B2B services:** Place of supply = customer's business location. If Wagner invoices a US business, no PT VAT; customer self-accounts under US rules.
- **B2C services:** Place of supply = supplier's location. If Wagner invoices US consumers (B2C), PT IVA applies at 23% (general rate) on top of the service price — Wagner must register for IVA, charge, file periodic returns.
- **Telecommunications, broadcasting, electronic services (B2C):** Since Jan 1, 2015 (EU VAT e-commerce reform), special place-of-supply rules apply. OSS (One-Stop-Shop) EU scheme lets Wagner register in one EU state for all B2C digital sales. **But Wagner is OUTSIDE the EU when invoicing from a US LLC — different rules.**

[PRACTICAL, PwC taxsummaries.pt 2026; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/portugal/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income]

**Practical scenario:**
- Wagner's US LLC invoices US clients → no PT VAT (B2B rule, customer outside PT)
- Wagner's US LLC invoices EU clients → PT VAT registration required (B2B with reverse charge; B2C with PT IVA)
- Wagner's US LLC invoices US consumers → no PT VAT unless Wagner establishes PT PE; subject to US sales tax

### 2.8 Banking & movement of money

**Three-tier banking strategy:**
1. **US bank** — keep for receiving USD revenue, US-side payments, USD cash flow. Common options: Mercury, Relay, Chase (Business Complete), Bank of America
2. **PT bank** — for local expenses (rent, utilities, taxes, payroll if any), and for receiving PT/customer payments. Common options: CGD (Caixa Geral de Depósitos), BCP (Millennium), Novo Banco, ActivoBank (digital-first, recommended for expats)
3. **Cross-border transfer service** — for moving money between US and PT bank accounts
   - **Wise (TransferWise)** — best rate for one-off transfers; multi-currency account (USD/EUR)
   - **Revolut Business / N26** — modern, but limited business banking
   - **Mercury** — US-based fintech with no fees for incoming wires (good for US business receiving from clients)
   - **Traditional bank wires** — most expensive, slowest, worst FX rate

**FBAR (FinCEN Form 114) [OFFICIAL + PRACTICAL]:**
- Any US person (citizen, resident, LLC owner) with foreign financial accounts whose aggregate maximum value during the year exceeds **$10,000** must file FBAR
- Filed separately from 1040, due April 15 (auto-extension to October 15)
- Filed electronically via BSA E-Filing System
- **[ACTION]** Wagner's PT bank account + Wise multi-currency account likely trigger FBAR once combined balance exceeds $10K. Likely Leo & father also trigger.
- Penalties: non-willful ~$16,536 per violation (2026 inflation-adjusted); willful ~$165,353 or 50% of account balance

**Form 8938 (FATCA) [OFFICIAL]:**
- Filed WITH Form 1040
- For US citizens **residing abroad**:
  - Single: $200,000 end-of-year OR $300,000 at any time
  - MFJ: $400,000 end-of-year OR $600,000 at any time
- Higher thresholds than US-resident filers
- For seniors (father/Leo) without substantial assets, often below threshold — but verify per person

**CRS (Common Reporting Standard) automatic exchange [OFFICIAL]:**
- PT banks automatically report foreign-resident account holders to PT's tax authority (AT), which exchanges with the account holder's residence state
- US is NOT a CRS participant (it uses FATCA reciprocally)
- **Implication:** PT reports US-citizen PT account data to PT AT; PT AT has no reciprocal exchange with the IRS, so PT bank data does NOT auto-flow to IRS. IRS gets data only via:
  1. FATCA (US-PT IGA — PT banks report US-owned accounts directly to IRS)
  2. Wagner's voluntary disclosure on FBAR + Form 8938 + Form 1040 Schedule B
