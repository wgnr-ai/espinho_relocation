## Q2 — Passport renewal process (US consulate route)

1. **Booking:** online only, MNE platform: https://agendamentosonline.mne.gov.pt/AgendamentosOnline/index.jsf (portal: agendamentos.mne.gov.pt). DC slots for the following month are released no later than the 10th of the preceding month; Palm Coast slots release daily. Must click "Submeter Agendamento" and receive a full confirmation email (a mere "registo online" email = NOT confirmed). No "ato familiar": book each document under each person's own name. [Source: Make an appointment, Embaixada Washington DC, retrieved 2026-09-02; FAQ idem]
2. **Documents (renewal):** valid Cartão de Cidadão (or BI) **and** the previous passport. **If the CC is expired, it must be renewed first** — "será necessário renovar primeiro o seu cartão de cidadão." Proof of US residence (e.g., US driver's license) recommended if possible. [Source: Passaporte Eletrônico Português service page, Embaixada Washington DC, retrieved 2026-09-02, https://washingtondc.embaixadaportugal.mne.gov.pt/pt/seccao-consular/servicos-consulares/passaporte-eletronico-portugues; same rule nationally: justica.gov.pt/Registos/Identificacao/Passaporte-eletronico (updated 2026-05-28), via delegated verification 2026-09-02]
3. **Photos:** no photo-to-bring requirement stated on the official DC passport page (biometrics captured at the appointment). 2 photos ARE required for emergency docs (TVU/passaporte temporário). [Source: ibid.; portaldascomunidades Outros Documentos de Viagem, retrieved 2026-09-02]
4. **Fees (September 2026 USD table, official):** passport delivered at consulate $87.61 (€75); urgent $140.18 (€120); passport mailed to user's address $122.66 (€105); temporary passport (6-month validity) $175.22 (€150); CC normal ≤5y $23.37 (€20); CC normal ≤10y $26.87 (€23). [Source: Tabela de Emolumentos Consulares USD — setembro 2026, Embaixada Washington DC/MNE, retrieved 2026-09-02, https://washingtondc.embaixadaportugal.mne.gov.pt/images/setembro-2026.pdf; EUR master table Portaria 229/2021: /images/seccao_consular/anexo_2_-_tabela_de_emolumentos_consulares.pdf]
5. **Payment:** in person — cash, card, or Money Order; by mail — Money Order only, payable to "Embassy of the Portuguese Republic". Monthly FX revisions apply. [Source: Custos de serviços, Embaixada Washington DC, retrieved 2026-09-02]
6. **Processing/delivery:** US delivery of a normal passport can take up to ~4 weeks/30 days. Two delivery options exist (pickup at post vs mailed to home — distinct fee lines above). [Source: gov.pt pedir-o-passaporte-eletronico-portugues + justica.gov.pt, retrieved 2026-09-02 via delegated verification]

## Q3 — Mobile/itinerant consular services ("presenças consulares")

1. The DC Embassy runs presenças in **Chicago, Houston, Manassas, and Miami**. Miami venue: Ocean Bank Center, 780 NW Le Jeune Rd, Suite 200, Miami, FL 33126. **No Orlando sessions exist.** [Source: Presenças Consulares, Embaixada Washington DC, retrieved 2026-09-02, https://washingtondc.embaixadaportugal.mne.gov.pt/pt/seccao-consular/presencas-consulares]
2. **2026 calendar (first 10 months):** Miami Feb 23-27 (done); Miami Mar 23-27 (done); Houston Jun 15-26; Miami Jun 25-26; Chicago Jul 6-10; Miami Aug 10-21; **Miami Oct 12-16 — registrations open until Sep 15, 2026**; Manassas Oct 23; Houston Nov 16-20 (slots released Oct 21). [Source: same page, retrieved 2026-09-02]
3. **Booking rules:** announced on the embassy site; appointments released up to 30 days before the session; strictly first-come-first-served; no waitlists; **one reservation per person** (duplicates auto-cancelled); confirm proposed time within 48h. Historically via presencas.washington@mne.pt + user info form; 2026 pages now point to registration platforms. [Source: same page + Consular Outreach EN page, retrieved 2026-09-02]
4. TVU/ETD emergency documents can also be issued at Houston/Miami presença locations. [Source: Embaixada Washington DC homepage notice (Houston 15-26 Jun / Miami sessions), retrieved 2026-09-02 via search index of https://washingtondc.embaixadaportugal.mne.gov.pt/pt/]

## Q4 — Alternatives

### (a) Renew during a visit to Portugal
1. Passport-capable IRN balcões in the area: **Registo Civil de Espinho** (Palácio da Justiça, Av. 24, 4501-851; CC + Passaporte; úteis 09h–16h); **Registo de Aveiro na Loja de Cidadão de Aveiro** (Rua Dr. Orlando Oliveira 41-47; CC + Passaporte; úteis 08h30–19h30 + Sat 09h30–15h00); Registo Civil de Santa Maria da Feira (Passaporte listed; CC NOT listed as of retrieval). [Source: IRN Contactos do Registo pages, irn.justica.gov.pt, retrieved 2026-09-02 via delegated verification]
2. Appointments: SIGA (agendamento.irn.mj.pt / SigaApp) — "qualquer pessoa pode agendar" (no residency bar); same-day senhas possible but conditioned by load; airport shops (Lisboa/Porto) by email appointment. [Source: justica.gov.pt/servicos/agendar-pedido-do-passaporte (updated 2026-01-15) + gov.pt, retrieved 2026-09-02 via delegated verification]
3. Fees/lead times in Portugal: normal 65€ / 5 dias úteis; expresso 85€ / 2 dias úteis; urgente 95€ / 1 dia útil; urgente Aeroporto de Lisboa same-day 100€; +40€ if old passport not surrendered. [Source: gov.pt service page (updated 2026-05-28), retrieved 2026-09-02 via delegated verification]
4. **Critical caveat:** a VALID CC is required for the passport there too; expired CC must be renewed first (~7 business days normal CC lead time) → a single short visit is not enough if the CC is also expired, unless urgent CC channels are used. [Source: justica.gov.pt + gov.pt/servicos/renovar-o-cartao-de-cidadao (updated 2026-02-10), retrieved 2026-09-02 via delegated verification]

### (b) Entry on an expired passport / emergency documents
1. Official standard: EU/PT citizens enter Portugal with a **valid** passport or valid ID; no official rule found authorizing entry on an expired passport. The citizen cannot be expelled/refused on document grounds alone without being given the chance to prove nationality, but boarding is the practical blocker. [Source: gov.pt/guias/entrar-e-sair-de-portugal (updated 2026-04-30) + europa.eu Your Europe, retrieved 2026-09-02 via delegated verification]
2. Airlines verify via IATA TIMATIC and normally refuse boarding on expired passports [PRACTICAL — industry practice; TIMATIC text not directly retrievable → source-level UNVERIFIED]. US Embassy Lisbon states travelers arriving without a valid passport will NOT be permitted to enter. [Source: pt.usembassy.gov/travelling-abroad, retrieved 2026-09-02]
3. **TVU (Título de Viagem Única):** return-to-Portugal-only emergency doc; 30€; accepts Portuguese ID "mesmo que expirado" (even expired) + 2 photos + proof of travel. [Source: boston.consuladoportugal.mne.gov.pt + newark.consuladoportugal.mne.gov.pt TVU pages + Portaria 229/2021 table, retrieved 2026-09-02 via delegated verification]
4. **Passaporte temporário:** up to 1-year validity full travel doc; 150€ ($175.22 in Sept 2026 US table); urgent/exceptional grounds; 2 photos + proof of travel. [Source: portaldascomunidades Outros Documentos de Viagem + Diário da República consolidated Portaria + setembro-2026.pdf, retrieved 2026-09-02]
