## 7. Elder-care add-on (Leo — standard senior needs)

- Centro de dia (IPSS / Segurança Social co-funded): **€150–€300/month**, means-tested on senior + family income. Includes meals, activities, hygiene support, transport. [PRACTICAL, Guia Sênior (2024-10, older — reverify at enrollment)] [S23]
- Local provider confirmed: Santa Casa da Misericórdia de Espinho runs Lar + Centro de Dia (capacity ~110 ERPI; day-center vacancy status varies). Pricing on inquiry. [PRACTICAL, SCME activity report + Apoio Perto directory, 2026-09-02] [S24]
- Serviço de Apoio Domiciliário (SAD): "valor sob consulta" via Segurança Social. [OFFICIAL, gov.pt service page] [S25]

## 8. Leisure / dining buffer

- National comfortable-couple dining/leisure: €300–€700/mo (guide range); modest €120–€300. Espinho price level ≈ Porto metro, below Lisbon. Coffee €0.70–€1.30; prato do dia €7–€10. [PRACTICAL, Simpsons 2026-06 + Unlock Portugal 2026-04] [S14, S9]
