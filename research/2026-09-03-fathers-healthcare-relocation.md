## (c) The Natera Signatera Problem

### C.1 The hard fact: Natera is the only Signatera test

27. **Signatera is Natera's proprietary product** — a personalized, tumor-informed ctDNA assay that requires the patient's original tumor tissue + matched normal DNA for whole-exome sequencing, then a custom mPCR-NGS panel is designed and run only at Natera's CLIA-certified lab in **Austin, Texas**. There is **no EU-based Signatera test**. Natera has clinical-trial and conference presence in Europe (CB & CDx Summit Europe 2026) but the Signatera assay itself is US-only. **[OFFICIAL, Natera Signatera FAQ + CDx Europe sponsor page + Natera press 2025-04-24, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://www.natera.com/oncology/signatera-advanced-cancer-detection/faq/ and https://cdx-europe.com/sponsor/natera/]**
28. Switching monitoring modality means moving to a **different test** (e.g., Guardant Reveal, Foundation Medicine, academic ctDNA program). It does NOT mean switching to a Signatera equivalent — no other vendor runs the same tumor-informed personalized MRD test. **[PRACTICAL, comparative ctDNA modality landscape, retrieved 2026-09-03]**

### C.2 Option 1: Continue Natera Signatera from Portugal

29. Natera explicitly serves patients outside the US. From Colontown University's FAQ on Signatera: "If I live outside of the US, can I order Signatera? Absolutely! Natera's patient coordinators can connect you with local representatives." **[PRACTICAL, Colontown University 2024-08-23, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://learn.colontown.org/topic/how-do-i-get-tested/]**
30. Process for an Espinho-based patient:
    - US-based oncology provider must remain the ordering physician (Signatera is ordered through a US Natera account).
    - Natera ships a **blood collection kit** (Streck Cell-Free DNA BCT® tan-top tubes × 2 + EDTA lavender-top × 1) to the patient via international courier, with prepaid FedEx Clinical Pak return label to **201 Industrial Road, Suite 410, San Carlos, CA 94070**.
    - Phlebotomy can be done locally — at any clinic that stocks Streck tubes (ULSGE laboratories can supply; private labs in Espinho / Gaia / Porto can also do this).
    - Sample stability: Streck tubes stable **9 days at room temperature**; lavender EDTA 14 days. Sufficient window for Monday-Monday Porto→San Carlos shipping.
    - TAT: 3–4 weeks from lab receipt for ongoing tests (1 week for plasma-only after initial design); initial tumor-tissue + whole-blood setup takes 2–3 weeks once tissue received.
    - Patient portal access is global; results delivered via Natera's portal to the ordering physician.
    - **[OFFICIAL/PRACTICAL, Natera Standing Order Sheet + UW Lab Medicine sendout protocols + Colontown FAQ, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://www.natera.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Signatera-Standing-Order-Sheet-1.pdf and https://testguide.labmed.uw.edu/view/RSIGNA]**
31. **Cost reality**: at 91, the father is likely on Medicare (US). Signatera is covered by Medicare for CRC, MIBC, breast, NSCLC, ovarian cancer in surveillance; other indications require an Advance Beneficiary Notice (ABN). The gastric-cancer indication is NOT in the Medicare-covered list. Out-of-pocket: typically $390–$950 per test depending on indication; Signatera offers financial-assistance programs. **[OFFICIAL, Natera Signatera Billing page, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://www.natera.com/oncology/billing/]**

### C.3 Option 2: Switch to EU-based MRD / liquid-biopsy monitoring

32. **Guardant Health EU** offers **Guardant Reveal** — a blood-based ctDNA test for MRD detection and recurrence monitoring in early-stage colorectal, breast, and lung cancers, plus therapy-response monitoring in advanced cancer. Distributed in Portugal by **Codex Genetics**. **[OFFICIAL/PRACTICAL, Guardant Health AMEA + Codex Genetics, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://www.guardanthealthamea.com/products/tests-for-patients-with-early-and-advanced-stage-cancer/ and https://www.codexgenetics.com/guardant-health/]**
33. **Guardant360** is also available through Codex Genetics PT for advanced solid-tumor profiling (not MRD — different use case). TAT 7 days. **[OFFICIAL, Guardant360 product page, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://guardanthealth.eu/]**
34. **Clinical equivalence reality**: Guardant Reveal is a tumor-*naive* (tumor-uninformed) ctDNA assay using a fixed panel covering common cancer genes + methylation. Signatera is tumor-*informed* (custom designed per patient). For MRD sensitivity in gastric cancer — where there is no standardized panel and tumor-informed tests historically outperform tumor-naive — **switching from Signatera to Guardant Reveal is a real clinical downgrade**. The father's US oncology team should be the one to evaluate this swap, not a Portuguese GP. **[PRACTICAL, comparative ctDNA modality assessment, retrieved 2026-09-03 — confirmed by Guardant AMEA product scope excluding gastric in current Reveal indications]**
35. **Foundation Medicine / Inivata / NEOgenomics**: Foundation Medicine is US-only (FoundationOne CDx; FoundationOne Liquid CDx is US-only). Inivata's InVisionFirst was acquired by NeoGenomics — also US-based. **No commercial EU-based tumor-informed MRD test for gastric cancer exists as of 2026-09-03.** **[PRACTICAL, ctDNA vendor landscape, retrieved 2026-09-03]**
36. **Academic ctDNA programs at IPO Porto / FMUP**: IPO Porto's CI-IPOP research center runs investigator-initiated trials and early-phase clinical trials including ctDNA-based studies (over 150 active trials; CI-IPOP is one of Portugal's strongest translational oncology units). **[OFFICIAL, IPO Porto research profile via oncoscreen.health, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://oncoscreen.health/oncology-institute-of-porto/]** For the father, an academic ctDNA program is a possibility but requires enrolment in a trial — slow, conditional, and not equivalent to quarterly Signatera monitoring.

### C.4 Recommendation

37. **Continue Natera Signatera from Portugal** is the lowest-risk path. The logistics (international kit, local phlebotomy, FedEx return) are well-documented. The clinical continuity benefit — same test, same assay design, same quantitative tracking — outweighs the modest added friction of shipping blood from Porto to San Carlos quarterly. **[PRACTICAL, synthesis from Natera Standing Order Sheet + Colontown FAQ, retrieved 2026-09-03]**

### D.2 Wait time reality for first oncology consultation (2026)

38. **TMRG for first oncology consultation (Post-April 2026 SINACC rules)**: only 2 priority levels — "muito prioritário" 7 days, "prioritário" 30 days. **[OFFICIAL, Portaria 135/2026/1, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/1s/2026/03/06300/0000800019.pdf]**
39. **Reality of compliance**: at end of 2025, **8,874 patients** were on the waiting list for first oncology consultation in PT public hospitals; **65.5% exceeded the legal max** (down from 78.6% in 2024). Source: ERS, reported in Observador + Portugal Post + SAPO. **[PRACTICAL, Observador 2026-08-13 + Portugal Post 2026-05-12, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://observador.pt/2026/08/13/sns-em-alguns-hospitais-mais-de-60-das-primeiras-consultas-de-especialidade-sao-realizadas-fora-do-tempo-maximo/ and https://theportugalpost.com/posts/cancer-surgery-wait-times-extend-as-portugals-public-healthcare-struggles]**
40. **IPO Porto specifically**: in late 2025 there were 4,933 patients on the oncology first-consultation list at IPO, 71.1% over legal time. Even "muito prioritário" cases had 70.8% exceeding the 7-day limit. **[PRACTICAL, SIC Notícias Verifica 2026-05-08, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://sicnoticias.pt/sicverifica/2026-05-08-video-mais-de-metade-das-primeiras-consultas-de-oncologia-falham-prazos-legais-como-disse-a-socialista-irene-costa--890db781]**

### D.3 How a returning senior jumps the waitlist

42. **Priority designation** (muito prioritário 7 days vs prioritário 30 days) is decided by the **referring physician**, not the patient. A US oncology summary letter that documents recent cancer treatment + active surveillance need with ctDNA monitoring is the strongest evidence for "muito prioritário" classification. **[OFFICIAL, Portaria 135/2026/1, retrieved 2026-09-03]**
43. **Externalização (SINACC mechanism)**: if a hospital cannot meet TMRG, the system auto-flags the patient for externalization to another unit with capacity — requires patient consent. In Espinho's geography, this could route from IPO Porto to ULSEDV or to a private-sector provider under SNS convention. **[OFFICIAL, Portaria 135/2026/1, retrieved 2026-09-03]**
44. **Practical bypass**: many returning patients with cancer history use a **private hospital first consultation** (CUF Porto, Hospital da Luz Porto, Trofa Saúde) to get an immediate assessment and a complete diagnostic plan; the SNS referral then comes with the private specialist's letter attached, which usually fast-tracks the SNS pathway. Cost of a private first oncology consult: €85–€200 (varies by hospital). **[PRACTICAL, Douro Centro Médico price page 2025-08-19 + CUF service catalog, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://dourocentromedico.pt/especialidades/gastrenterologia/]**
45. **IPO Porto transport subsidy**: under a 2026-08-05 protocol with Rede Expressos, IPO Porto patients with confirmed oncology diagnosis + planned surgery/radiotherapy/chemo can travel **Porto (Polo Universitário) ⇄ destination at €6/ticket** (patient + 1 companion). A declaration from IPO Porto is required, bought at the Rodonorte store near the IPO. **[OFFICIAL, HR Portugal 2026-08-05, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://hrportugal.sapo.pt/utentes-em-tratamento-no-ipo-porto-podem-viajar-na-rede-expresso-por-6-euros/]** Espinho → Porto (Campanhã) is also only ~30 min by CP regional train.

## (e) Private Insurance for a 91-Year-Old Senior with Cancer History

### E.1 The hard reality

53. **Standard Portuguese private health insurers cap entry age between 55 and 65**. Verified contracts:
    - **Multicare 1|2|3 (Fidelidade)**: max inclusion age **60**; no age limit for permanence, but cancer-specific coverage ends at 70. **[OFFICIAL, Fidelidade IPC Multicare 1|2|3 PDF, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://www.fidelidade.pt/PT/particulares/Saude/seguros2016/123/Documents/seguro-saude-1-2-3/IPC+DIPS%20Seguro%20de%20Sa%C3%BAde%20Multicare%201-2-3%20Individual_1jul2025.pdf]**
    - **Multicare Proteção Padrão (Fidelidade)**: max inclusion age **60**. **[OFFICIAL, Multicare Proteção Padrão PDF, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://multicare.pt/hubfs/2025-Multicare/Documentacao/protecao-padrao/info_pre_contratual_padrao.pdf]**
    - **Multicare Proteção Vital (Fidelidade, oncology-focused)**: max inclusion age **65**, but **explicitly excludes** "pessoas que tenham tido ou tenham suspeitas de ter doença oncológica" — the father cannot be insured under this product regardless of age. **[OFFICIAL, Multicare Proteção Vital IPC PDF, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://www.fidelidade.pt/PT/particulares/Saude/seguros2016/oncologico/Documents/coberturas_servicos/IPC+DIPS_Seguro%20de%20Sa%C3%BAde%20Multicare%20Prote%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Vital.pdf]**
    - **Multicare 60+ (Fidelidade, the only "senior" Multicare product)**: inclusion age **61–70** only — the father at 91 is past this bracket. **[OFFICIAL, Multicare 60+ IPC PDF, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://www.fidelidade.pt/PT/particulares/Saude/seguros2016/60-mais/Documents/IPC%2001%20AGOSTO/IPC+DIPS%20Seguro%20de%20Sa%C3%BAde%20Individual%2060+%20Multicare_1jul2025.pdf]**
    - **Médis Vintage / Ageas Saúde Sénior**: subscription age 55–75 (from 2026-09-02 prior report).
    - **AdvanceCare / CUF**: typical commercial products cap at 60–65.
54. **Bottom line for a 91-year-old with cancer history**: **standard Portuguese commercial private health insurance is not available**. The father is ineligible by age for every Fidelidade / Multicare product, by age for every Médis product, and by pre-existing cancer history for the one product (Multicare Proteção Vital) whose age cap might have been negotiable.

### E.2 MGEN — the only viable Portuguese option

55. **MGEN (Mutuelle Générale de l'Éducation Nationale)** is a mutual insurer (not standard commercial) with structurally different rules: **no age limit for adhesion, no age limit for permanence, no medical questionnaire, no exclusion of pre-existing diseases (1-year carência), no exclusion of grave diseases**. **[OFFICIAL, Seguramos MGEN product page + MGEN mutualcare PDF, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://www.seguramos.pt/seguro-saude-mgen/ and https://www.mutualcare.pt/planos/ASDS_informacao_generica.pdf]**
56. MGEN coverage tiers (Hospitalization capital):
    - **Essencial**: hospitalization €30,000, ambulatory €1,000, estomatologia via Dentinet network
    - **Ideal**: hospitalization €60,000, ambulatory €2,500, estomatologia €500
    - **Total**: hospitalization €100,000, ambulatory €5,000, estomatologia €1,000 — **no co-payments on hospitalization/ambulatory**
    - **[OFFICIAL, Protev Seguros MGEN Empresas, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://protevseguros.pt/en/seguros-de-saude-para-empresas-mgen/]**

## (f) Practical Continuity of US Prescriptions

### F.1 The legal fact

64. **US prescriptions are not valid in Portuguese pharmacies**. A PT-licensed physician must rewrite them. EU cross-border prescription rules (Diretiva 2011/24/UE + Diretiva de Execução 2012/52/UE) apply for prescriptions written by EU physicians; US prescriptions are not covered. **[OFFICIAL, Your Europe / EU Commission "Aviar uma receita médica noutro país da UE", retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/health/prescription-medicine-abroad/prescriptions/index_pt.htm and https://www.sns24.gov.pt/en/servico/receitas-e-medicamentos-na-europa/]**
65. Medication names differ between US and PT/EEA. Active substance (INN) name is the standard for cross-border recognition. A pharmacist may substitute the prescribed drug with an equivalent containing the same active substance, dose, and pharmaceutical form if the exact brand is unavailable locally. **[OFFICIAL, Your Europe cross-border prescription FAQ, retrieved 2026-09-03]**

### F.2 Process for the father's prescriptions

66. **Step 1**: Compile a US-side medical summary before departure, including all current medications (active substance + brand name + dose + indication), prior cancer treatment summaries (surgery, chemo, radiation, current surveillance plan), most recent Signatera results, most recent imaging (CT / PET) dates and findings.
67. **Step 2**: Use a **sworn translator** (tradutor juramentado) certified for medical translation — Alphatrad (PT branch with 40+ years of experience, 80 centers worldwide) is a typical reference. **Cost: €50–€150 per page** depending on complexity and urgency. Some translations qualify for "urgent" handling in 24–48 hours. **[PRACTICAL, Alphatrad PT page 2026-07-30, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://alphatrad.pt/noticias/traducao-de-receitas-medicas]**
68. **Step 3**: At the first SNS GP appointment at UCSP Espinho, present the translated US medical summary. The GP rewrites all prescriptions in the SNS PEM (prescrição eletrónica médica) format — these then dispense at any Portuguese pharmacy at the standard SNS reimbursement rate. **[OFFICIAL, SNS receita eletrónica, retrieved 2026-09-03]**
69. **Step 4**: For medications not available in PT (rare; almost all common US drugs have PT equivalents), the GP refers to a hospital pharmacy or imports via INFARMED's medicamento de uso humano não disponível procedure — this is an established pathway for unavailable drugs. **[OFFICIAL, INFARMED Prescrição norms PDF, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://www.infarmed.pt/documents/15786/17838/Normas_Prescri%C3%A7%C3%A3o/bcd0b378-3b00-4ee0-9104-28d0db0b7872]**

### F.3 Translation & continuity services

70. **CUF Internacional**: physical support at CUF Descobertas, CUF Porto, CUF Tejo, CUF Cascais. English + Spanish support. Helps with: appointment booking, second opinions, cost estimates, payment guarantees from international insurers. **[OFFICIAL, CUF Internacional page, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://www.cuf.pt/servicos-cuf/internacional-cuf]**

Helps international patients access care. **[OFFICIAL, Hospital da Luz international patient pages, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://www.hospitaldaluz.pt/santamariafeira/en/for-clients/international-patients]**

Cost: ~€200–€400 first consult, ~€80 translation support, ~€50 per page sworn translation. **[UNVERIFIED for specific CUF translation fees — request quote from CUF Internacional directly]**

## (g) The Expats-Returning-After-Years-Abroad Medicare Coordination Note

### G.1 Medicare does not cover care in Portugal

73. Medicare explicitly excludes coverage outside the US (with very narrow exceptions — e.g., a US hospital closer to a PT residence than the nearest US hospital for an emergency, or on a cruise ship within 6 hours of a US port). Routine care in Portugal is **not** covered. **[OFFICIAL, CMS Medicare.gov coverage rules, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://www.medicare.gov/basics/costs/medicare-costs/avoid-penalties]**

### G.2 The Part B late-enrollment penalty

74. The Part B late-enrollment penalty is **10% of the standard Part B premium for each full 12-month period the beneficiary could have had Part B but did not**. It lasts **as long as Part B is held** — i.e., permanent.
    - **2026 standard Part B premium**: $202.90/month
    - Penalty accrual examples (per 3tej analysis 2026-08-09):
      - 2 years abroad → +20% → +$40.58/month = $243.48/month total
      - 5 years abroad → +50% → +$101.45/month = $304.35/month total
      - 10 years abroad → +100% → premium doubled to $405.80/month, permanent
    - **[OFFICIAL, CMS / Medicare.gov 2026 calculations, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://www.medicare.gov/basics/costs/medicare-costs/avoid-penalties]**
    - **[PRACTICAL, 3tej Medicare Abroad 2026-08-09 + AARP Medicare penalties 2026-05-02, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://3tej.com/blog/medicare-when-you-retire-abroad and https://www.aarp.org/medicare/how-much-is-the-part-b-late-enrollment-penalty/]**

### G.3 The CMS-1763 suspension/deactivation process

75. **Form CMS-1763** ("Request for Termination of Premium Part A, Part B, or Part B Immunosuppressive Drug Coverage") — current version 01/26 (expires 2027-08-31). Must be:
    - Signed by the enrollee (or representative with documentation)
    - Witnessed by 2 people who watched the enrollee sign
    - Submitted by **mail or fax to the local SSA office** (find via SSA.gov/locator)
    - Specify the desired coverage end month (coverage ends **last day of the month AFTER** submission)
    - Include a written reason for termination
    - Return the Medicare card (if dropping Part A or Part B; replacement card issued if keeping Part A)
    - **[OFFICIAL, CMS CMS-1763 PDF + Healthgrades 2025-10-31 + Healthline 2024-09-03, retrieved 2026-09-03 — https://www.cms.gov/medicare/cms-forms/cms-forms/downloads/cms1763.pdf and https://resources.healthgrades.com/right-care/medicare/how-to-cancel-medicare-part-b]**

### G.4 Recommendation

76. **Keep Part B active** during the 2026–2027 transition year. The annual cost (~$2,435/year at 2026 premium) is small relative to the permanent penalty risk if the father decides to return to the US for any reason. **[PRACTICAL, 3tej analysis 2026-08-09, retrieved 2026-09-03]**
77. After PT residency is well-established (suggest 12–18 months in Espinho), and if the family is confident no US return is planned, **submit CMS-1763** to drop Part B. The 12–18 month buffer ensures no accidental overpayment during the transition and gives time for any unexpected US visits. **[PRACTICAL, synthesis from CMS + 3tej + AARP, retrieved 2026-09-03]**
78. **Part D (prescription drug coverage)**: not relevant for the father's day-to-day (PT pharmacy system will be primary), but suspending Part D does not incur penalty if he never enrolls again — only if he wants to enroll after 63 days without creditable coverage. Keep Part D if there's any US travel expected in the transition year.
79. **Medicare Advantage (Part C)**: typically not relevant for a retiree outside the US — most MA plans require residence in the plan's service area.
