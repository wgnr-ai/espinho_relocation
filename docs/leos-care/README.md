## Key Findings (see research report for citations)

- **EU 2026/131 (non-commercial) and EU 2026/848 (commercial) regulations took effect 22 April 2026.** Move (Apr–Jun 2027) is firmly in the new regime. EU pet passports are no longer valid for non-EU residents entering the EU; the new EU 2026/131 health certificate format is mandatory.
- **Tapeworm treatment is NOT required for Portugal** (only Finland, Malta, Ireland, Norway).
- **Microchip (ISO 15-digit) must be implanted BEFORE the rabies vaccination** — the single most common and costly sequencing mistake.
- **Trupanion (US-based) explicitly limits coverage to US/Canada/Puerto Rico/Australia** — must be cancelled or paused before relocation. Trupanion cannot be continued in PT.
- **Decreto-Lei n.º 315/2009** mandates third-party liability insurance for breeds classified as potentially dangerous. Confirm Leo's breed before applying.
- **SIAC registration is mandatory for all dogs in Portugal** and is required by every PT pet insurer.
- **United Airlines PetSafe cargo is closed to civilians in 2026** — only cabin travel ($150/way, ≤20 lb carrier) is available. A medium-large dog cannot fly United in cabin. TAP Cargo, Delta Cargo, AA Cargo, and IPATA-vetted pet-transport specialists are the realistic options for Leo.
