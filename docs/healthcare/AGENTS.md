## Work Guidance

- **Father's profile (subject of 2026-09-03 report)**: 91 years old (turns 91 Oct 2026), prior lung / prostate / gastric cancer treated, MI 1996, currently on quarterly Natera Signatera liquid-biopsy ctDNA monitoring (all negative to date). Relocation target Apr–Jun 2027.
- **SNS registration baseline**: UCSP Espinho (Unidade Local de Saúde de Gaia/Espinho, E.P.E.); SNS is universal for PT citizens regardless of age or pre-existing conditions. Do NOT pursue private insurance as primary — the father's cancer history disqualifies him from all standard commercial products (Multicare 1|2|3 max age 60; Multicare 60+ max age 70; Multicare Proteção Vital explicitly excludes cancer history; Médis Vintage max 75). The only viable Portuguese private option is **MGEN** (mutualist, no age limit, no pre-existing exclusion, 1-year carência).
- **Specialist pathway**: UCSP Espinho GP → ULSGE Hospital Nossa Senhora da Ajuda (local Espinho) → ULSEDV Hospital de São Sebastião (Santa Maria da Feira, 15 km from Espinho, has Hospital de Dia Médico – Oncologia + European Gold Badge 2026) → IPO Porto (~30 km, national oncology reference, ~30 min CP train from Espinho). For gastric cancer specifically, IPO Porto's Digestive Pathology Clinic is the national reference (Prof. Dr. Mário Dinis Ribeiro, Bial Prize winner).
- **Natera Signatera logistics**: continued from Portugal is feasible (Natera ships kits internationally, US-based lab in San Carlos CA, Streck tubes stable 9 days at room temp, FedEx return). Signatera MUST be ordered by US oncology provider — cannot transfer to PT physician.
- **Prescription continuity**: US prescriptions are NOT valid in PT pharmacies (EU cross-border directive does not cover US). A SNS physician must rewrite. Pre-arrival translation (Alphatrad, €50–€150/page) is the practical bridge. Bring 3 months of medications in original US bottles to bridge the gap.
- **Medicare Part B**: keep active through transition year (cost ~$2,435/year at 2026 premium) — penalty is 10%/year of gap, permanent. Submit CMS-1763 only after 12–18 months stable in PT and if no US return planned.
- **Hospital São Sebastião (Santa Maria da Feira)** is no longer the right entity framing — correct current state is **ULS Entre Douro e Vouga (ULSEDV)** for Hospital de São Sebastião, while Espinho's hospital (**Hospital Nossa Senhora da Ajuda**) is part of **ULSGE**. Do NOT cite 2009-era CHEDV text claiming Hospital de Espinho belongs to ULSEDV.
