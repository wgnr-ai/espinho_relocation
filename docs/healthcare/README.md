# Healthcare

## Overview

Navigating the Portuguese healthcare system for Wagner (general adult) and his **father — a 91-year-old PT citizen (turns 91 Oct 2026) with prior lung / prostate / gastric cancer treated, MI 1996, currently on quarterly Natera Signatera liquid-biopsy monitoring (all negative to date). Relocation target Apr–Jun 2027.**

Leo (the dog) is a separate workstream at `docs/leos-care/` — veterinary, not human, healthcare.

## Research Questions

- SNS (Serviço Nacional de Saúde) enrollment process for returning PT citizens — age + cancer history do not block access
- Family doctor assignment via UCSP Espinho — likely on waiting list (300% rise in patients without doctor in ULSGE 2025–2026)
- Gastric cancer MDT pathway: IPO Porto Digestive Pathology Clinic (national reference)
- Lung cancer follow-up: local Pneumologia at Hospital Nossa Senhora da Ajuda + annual low-dose CT at Espinho
- Prostate cancer follow-up: PSA + urology at ULSGE (Gaia) or ULSEDV (Santa Maria da Feira)
- Cardiac care (post-MI 1996): Cardiologia de Gaia + ECG/echo locally
- Natera Signatera continued from Portugal (US lab, FedEx logistics, requires US oncology provider)
- Private insurance for 91-year-old with cancer history — only MGEN is viable in PT
- US prescription translation and continuity
- Medicare Part B — keep active through transition year (avoid 10%/year permanent penalty)
- Pre-arrival translation, sworn translation services (Alphatrad)
- IPO Porto first-appointment wait time reality (TMRG 7 days "muito prioritário", but 70%+ exceed it in practice)

## Key Findings (synthesized from research/2026-09-03-fathers-healthcare-relocation.md)

1. **SNS universal access confirmed.** No AIMA / SEF / visa for PT citizens. Father registers at UCSP Espinho (Unidade Local de Saúde de Gaia/Espinho, E.P.E.) at Rua 37, 4500-330 Espinho, tel 227 331 130.
2. **Cancer care pathway**: GP at UCSP Espinho → ULSGE Hospital Nossa Senhora da Ajuda (local Espinho, has Cardiologia + Pneumologia + Gastrenterologia + new TAC) → ULSEDV Hospital de São Sebastião (Santa Maria da Feira, 15 km from Espinho, holds European Cancer Organisation Gold Badge 2026 + has Hospital de Dia Médico – Oncologia) → IPO Porto (Rua Dr. António Bernardino de Almeida 4200-072 Porto, ~30 km, ~30 min CP train from Espinho; central phone +351 225 084 000; digestive pathology clinic Director Prof. Dr. Mário Dinis Ribeiro).
3. **Natera Signatera**: Can be continued from Portugal. Process: US oncology provider orders, Natera ships Streck + EDTA kit to PT, local phlebotomy, FedEx return to San Carlos CA. TAT 3–4 weeks from lab receipt. Critical: tumor tissue must be available from US pathology (paraffin block from gastric surgery ~2 years ago). Medicare does NOT cover Signatera for gastric cancer indication (not in approved list); out-of-pocket ~$390–$950/test with Natera financial-assistance program.
4. **EU alternatives not equivalent**: Guardant Reveal (tumor-naive, lower sensitivity in gastric MRD); Foundation Medicine and Inivata/NeoGenomics are US-only. No commercial EU-based tumor-informed MRD test for gastric cancer exists. Continue Signatera.
5. **Private insurance**: All standard commercial products (Multicare 1|2|3 max age 60, Multicare 60+ max age 70, Multicare Proteção Vital explicitly excludes cancer history, Médis Vintage max 75) **disqualify the father**. **MGEN** is the only viable option — no age limit, no medical questionnaire, no pre-existing disease exclusion, 1-year carência. Hospitalization capital: €30k–€100k depending on plan (Essencial / Ideal / Total). Network: AdvanceCare (16,000 partners). Premium must be quoted (call Seguramos +351 228 347 910 or Protev Seguros).
6. **Prescription continuity**: US prescriptions are NOT valid in PT pharmacies. Practical bridge: pre-arrival sworn translation (Alphatrad, €50–€150/page) + 3 months of US medication supply in original bottles + first SNS GP appointment to rewrite prescriptions in PEM format.
7. **Medicare Part B**: Keep active through transition year. Cost ~$2,435/year at 2026 premium ($202.90/mo). Permanent penalty 10%/year of gap if dropped and re-enrolled. CMS-1763 form required to suspend. Suspend after 12–18 months stable in PT if no US return planned.
8. **IPO Porto first-appointment TMRG**: 7 days ("muito prioritário") or 30 days ("prioritário") under SINACC Portaria 135/2026/1. Real-world compliance ~30% — 70% of patients exceed. Use tempos.min-saude.pt portal to gauge live waits.
9. **Hospital São Sebastião (Santa Maria da Feira) is ULS Entre Douro e Vouga (ULSEDV), NOT ULSGE.** Espinho's Hospital Nossa Senhora da Ajuda is ULSGE. Historical CHEDV text claiming Hospital de Espinho belongs to ULSEDV is stale and incorrect.
